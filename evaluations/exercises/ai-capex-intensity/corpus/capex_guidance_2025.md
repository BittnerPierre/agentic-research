# Initial FY2025 Capex Guidance - Frozen as of February 7, 2025

This table records the first full-year 2025 guidance available after the companies'
FY2024 results. It is a historical snapshot, not the latest guidance. Later revisions
must not be substituted. Guidance is forward-looking and must remain separate from
reported FY2025 actuals.

| Company | Release date | Initial FY2025 guidance | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon | 2025-02-06 | approximately $100B | management estimate of capital expenditures |
| Alphabet | 2025-02-04 | approximately $75B | reported capex; cash-payment timing can cause variability |
| Meta | 2025-01-29 | $60B-$65B | includes principal payments on finance leases |

The corpus actual for Meta is cash paid to acquire property and equipment. It excludes
finance-lease principal, so a percentage variance against Meta's guidance is not
like-for-like and must not be calculated. The FY2025 Meta company-reported capex measure
including finance-lease principal was $72.215B ($69.691B cash PP&E plus $2.524B of
finance-lease principal).

No initial FY2025 capex guidance is provided in this frozen source pack for Microsoft,
NVIDIA, or Apple. Report it as unavailable rather than estimating it.
