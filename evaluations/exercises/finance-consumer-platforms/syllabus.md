# Financial Analysis Syllabus — Consumer Platform Unit-Economics Maturation

## Scenario

You are a senior analyst preparing an internal thematic note for an investment
committee on **how large-cap consumer internet platforms mature from a
growth-at-all-costs posture toward disciplined monetization and self-funded cash
generation (2019–2024)**. The committee wants a focused, decision-ready brief —
not a question/answer list, and not a company-by-company data dump.

The three platforms in scope are **Netflix, Airbnb, and Zillow**. Each illustrates
a different facet of the same maturation arc:
- **Netflix** — pricing power and ARPU expansion, then a deliberate plateau.
- **Airbnb** — monetization discipline (a stable take rate) while volume scales.
- **Zillow** — a cash-generation inflection after exiting a capital-intensive line.

## Deliverable — a chaptered brief with embedded data tables

Produce a structured markdown report. Each analytical chapter must combine a short

Required chapters:

1. **Executive Summary** — the thesis and the two or three key takeaways.
2. **Scope & Method** — companies, time window, which unit-economics metrics you
   track, and the limits of the evidence.
3. **Monetization & Unit Economics** — Netflix ARPU trajectory; Airbnb take rate
   and gross-booking-value per night. Narrative + table.
4. **Cash Generation & Capital Discipline** — Zillow free-cash-flow inflection;
   Netflix near-term cash requirements. Narrative + table.
5. **Cross-Platform Synthesis** — what "maturation" looks like across the three,
   and where they differ.
6. **Data Gaps & Follow-Up Questions** — what the sources do not support.

## Key questions the brief must answer

- How did **Netflix ARPU** evolve across 2019–2024, and what does the shape of that
  curve (expansion then plateau) imply about pricing power vs. mix?
- Is **Airbnb** expanding monetization by raising its **take rate**, or by growing
  **volume/value per night** at a stable take rate? What does the evidence show?
- What drove **Zillow's** swing from negative to strongly positive **FCF margin**,
  and is it structural or one-off?
- Taken together, what distinguishes a "maturing" consumer platform from a
  "land-grab" one, on the evidence provided?
- Where is the evidence insufficient to close the thesis?
