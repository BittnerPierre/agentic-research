### 13) Shift4 Payments (NYSE: FOUR) — Vendor Concentration Risk (as of Dec 31, 2024)

* **Bank sponsor dependence:** because Shift4 is not a member bank in North America, it relies on a **sponsorship agreement with a member bank**; if terminated for breach, Shift4 would have **180 days** to identify an alternative sponsor. ([Shift4][2])

**Offsetting diversification disclosures (context):**

* “no single merchant accounting for more than **3%** of revenue in recent years” ([Shift4][2])
