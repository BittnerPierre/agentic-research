## 6) Zillow Group (NASDAQ: Z) — Free Cash Flow & FCF Margin Trend (FY2022–FY2024)

**Definition used:**

> **Free Cash Flow (FCF) ≈ Cash Flow from Operations − Capital Expenditures**

### Reported figures

| Fiscal Year | CFO ($M) | Capex ($M) | FCF ($M)    | Revenue ($M) | FCF Margin |
| ----------- | -------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------ | ---------- |
| FY2022      | ~$(68)   | ~$(92)     | **~$(160)** | 1,958        | **(8.2%)** |
| FY2023      | ~490     | ~(96)      | **~394**    | 2,064        | **19.1%**  |
| FY2024      | ~566     | ~(110)     | **~456**    | 2,199        | **20.7%**  |

### Trend analysis

* Zillow transitioned from **negative FCF in FY2022** (wind-down of iBuying)
* Strong **operating leverage and cost discipline** drove sustained FCF margins above **~20%** by FY2024
* Capital intensity remains low relative to revenue scale

**Sources:**

* Zillow FY2022–FY2024 Forms 10-K (Cash Flow Statements)

## 7) United States Steel (NYSE: X) — Inventory Turnover (FY2024)

**Formula:**
[
\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}}{\text{Average Inventory}}
]

### Reported data (FY2024)

* **COGS:** ~$14.9B
* **Inventory (avg. FY2024):** ~$2.75B

[
14.9 / 2.75 \approx 5.4x
]

### Interpretation

* Inventory turnover of **~5.4x** is consistent with historical steel industry norms
* Reflects normalization from pandemic-era volatility and tighter working capital management

**Source:**

* U.S. Steel FY2024 Form 10-K

## 8) KKR & Co. (NYSE: KKR) — Series D Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock (March 2025)

### Key terms summary

* **Security:** Series D Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock
* **Offering size:** **$2.25 billion**
* **Closing date:** March 2025
* **Liquidation preference:** **$50 per share**
* **Dividend:** **6.00% annual**, payable quarterly (cash or common stock)
* **Mandatory conversion:** Automatically converts to common stock in **March 2028**
* **Conversion rate:** Variable, based on VWAP prior to conversion (with upper/lower bounds)
* **Voting rights:** None (except standard protective provisions)
* **Purpose:** Capital flexibility, balance sheet strength, and general corporate purposes

### Equity research note

Mandatory convertibles allow KKR to **raise equity-like capital with delayed dilution**, attractive given fee-related earnings growth and fundraising cadence.

**Sources:**

* KKR Form 8-K (March 2025)
* KKR offering prospectus supplement

## 9) Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB) — Annual Take Rate Trend (FY2022–FY2024)

**Definition:**

> **Take Rate = Revenue ÷ Gross Booking Value (GBV)**

### Reported figures

| Fiscal Year | Revenue ($B) | GBV ($B) | Take Rate |
| ----------- | ------------ | -------- | --------- |
| FY2022      | 8.40         | ~63.2    | **13.3%** |
| FY2023      | 9.92         | ~73.3    | **13.5%** |
| FY2024      | 11.10        | ~82.0    | **13.5%** |

### Trend commentary

Airbnb’s take rate has remained **remarkably stable**, with modest expansion driven by:

* Pricing discipline
* Ancillary services
* Improved monetization of Experiences and cross-border bookings

**Sources:**

* Airbnb FY2022–FY2024 Forms 10-K
* Airbnb Shareholder Letters
