## 1. United States Steel (NYSE: X) – Nippon Steel Merger Context

**Transaction Overview & Management Positioning**

U.S. Steel has consistently communicated that its **planned merger with Nippon Steel does not alter day-to-day business operations** while the transaction remains subject to regulatory and political review. Management has emphasized operational continuity for customers, suppliers, and employees throughout the approval process.

Despite delays and heightened regulatory scrutiny, U.S. Steel has continued to operate independently, making plant-level and capacity decisions without deferring actions due to merger timing uncertainty. The company has framed the transaction as a long-term strategic outcome rather than an operational dependency.

**Sources:**

* Nippon Steel press communications (March 2024)
* U.S. Steel / Nippon Steel public merger statements

## 2. Netflix (NASDAQ: NFLX) – Average Revenue Per Paying User (ARPU)

Netflix reports **“Average Monthly Revenue per Paying Membership”** as its ARPU metric.

| Year | ARPU ($) |
| ---- | -------- |
| 2019 | $10.82   |
| 2020 | $11.00   |
| 2021 | $11.32   |
| 2022 | $11.76   |
| 2023 | $11.64   |
| 2024 | $11.70   |

**Trend Summary:**
ARPU expanded steadily from 2019 through 2022, driven by pricing actions and mix shift. Growth plateaued in 2023 and remained broadly stable in 2024, reflecting a balance between pricing, ad-supported tier adoption, and geographic mix.

**Sources:**

* Netflix Form 10-K / 10-Q filings
* YCharts (compiled from SEC disclosures)

```
Q2 2024: $5.4B (low) to $6.0B (high), 10.5% of midpoint
Q3 2024: $6.4B (low) to $7.0B (high), 9.0% of midpoint
```
