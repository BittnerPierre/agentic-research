### 4) TSMC — Q1 2025 guidance check using Feb’25 actuals + historical March seasonality (showing work)

**Scenario framing (from your prompt):** As of **March 11, 2025**, February financials were released on **March 10, 2025**. ([pr.tsmc.com][4])

#### Step A — Q1 2025 revenue guidance (company outlook)

TSMC guided **Q1 2025 revenue: $25.0B to $25.8B**, assuming **US$1 = NT$32.8**. ([investor.tsmc.com][5])

* Midpoint = **$25.4B**

#### Step B — Known Jan & Feb 2025 revenue (NT$)

From the Feb 2025 monthly revenue release: ([pr.tsmc.com][4])

* **Jan–Feb 2025 total:** **NT$553.30B**
* **Feb 2025:** **NT$260.01B**
  ⇒ **Jan 2025 implied:** 553.30 − 260.01 = **NT$293.29B**

#### Step C — Historical March MoM growth rates (Feb → Mar) for the past 3 years

From TSMC March revenue releases (each states the % change from February):

* **Mar 2022:** **+17.0%** vs Feb ([tsmc.com][6])
* **Mar 2023:** **−10.9%** vs Feb ([Micron Technology][7])
* **Mar 2024:** **+7.5%** vs Feb ([d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net][8])

Average March MoM growth = (17.0% − 10.9% + 7.5%) / 3 = **~+4.53%**

#### Step D — Project March 2025 revenue using the average March MoM growth

* Projected **Mar 2025** = Feb 2025 × (1 + 4.53%)
  = 260.01 × 1.0453 ≈ **NT$271.79B** ([pr.tsmc.com][4])

#### Step E — Project implied Q1 2025 revenue (NT$) and convert using guidance FX assumption

* Implied **Q1 2025 NT$** = Jan 293.29 + Feb 260.01 + Mar 271.79
  = **NT$825.09B** ([pr.tsmc.com][4])
* Convert at **32.8 NT$/US$**: 825.09 / 32.8 ≈ **$25.15B** ([investor.tsmc.com][5])

**Conclusion (vs guidance):**

* Versus **midpoint ($25.4B)**: **MISS by ~$0.25B**
* Versus **guided range ($25.0B–$25.8B)**: **within range** ([investor.tsmc.com][5])
