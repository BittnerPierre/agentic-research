### 17) Paylocity (NASDAQ: PCTY) — Regulatory risks disclosed in FY2024 10-K

Paylocity flags that it and/or its clients operate under **complex and evolving regulatory regimes**, and that changes can require product modifications and increase compliance costs. Key regulatory risk themes include: ([SEC][4])

* **Data privacy / personal information regulation:** increasing U.S. state privacy laws and regimes (e.g., **CCPA/CPRA**, **BIPA**), plus **GDPR** exposure via customers with international operations. ([SEC][4])
* **Healthcare / benefits-related compliance:** includes **HIPAA** impacts (noted as relevant to benefits administration and the company’s self-insured group health plan). ([SEC][4])
* **Breach notification / enforcement risk:** higher enforcement activity and penalties; noncompliance can trigger **significant fines**, investigations, restrictions, and reputational harm. ([SEC][4])
* **Tax law uncertainty (including digital services):** new or changed **income/sales/use tax** rules can be complex, potentially retroactive, and may increase the cost of services and compliance burden. ([SEC][4])
* **Broader employment/benefits compliance complexity:** Paylocity notes its solutions help clients comply with **tax, benefit, and other laws**, which can change and force product/service updates. ([SEC][4])
