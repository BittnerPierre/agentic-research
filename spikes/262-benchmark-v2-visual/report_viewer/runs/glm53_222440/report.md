# Junior Analyst Data Pack – FY2025 AI Capex Intensity (Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Apple)

## Overview and Definitions

This data pack tracks seven metrics per company: revenue, operating income, operating margin, operating cash flow (OCF), capital expenditures (capex), free cash flow (FCF), and capex intensity [S2] [S4]. Operating margin is defined as operating income / revenue; FCF = OCF − capex; and capex intensity = capex / OCF [S2] [S4]. All figures are in US$ billions, drawn from each company's Form 10-K consolidated statements [S2] [S6].

Fiscal-year ends differ across the six companies and periods do not align, so all comparisons in this pack are made on a fiscal-year, not calendar, basis [S2] [S4] [S6]. Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta report fiscal years ending in December; Microsoft in June; NVIDIA in late January; and Apple in late September [S2] [S4].

FY2025 is identified as the latest fiscal year with a complete metric set for all six companies in the frozen corpus, and serves as the analysis reference year [S2] [S4] [S6]. FY2025 capex actuals range from NVIDIA's $3.2B to Amazon's $131.8B, with capex intensity from 5% (NVIDIA) to 94% (Amazon) [S4] [S6].

All six companies increased capex over the period, but the magnitude varied widely [S5][S4]. Amazon recorded the largest absolute increase (+$91.7B), with capex reaching $131.8B in FY2025 [S5]. Alphabet, Meta, and Microsoft each roughly quadrupled capex, from $22.3B, $15.2B, and $15.4B respectively in FY2020 [S5][S4]. NVIDIA, whose earliest available figure is FY2022 (no FY2020–FY2021 capex in the corpus), more than tripled from $1.0B to $3.2B [S5][S3]. Apple's growth was the most modest, from $7.3B to $12.7B [S5]. Figures are from SEC 10-K filings on a fiscal-year basis (Amazon/Alphabet/Meta: Dec; Microsoft: Jun; NVIDIA: late Jan; Apple: late Sep) [S5][S4]. Meta's figures reflect cash paid for property and equipment, excluding finance-lease principal [S5].

Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Apple initial FY2025 capex guidance must be reported as unavailable rather than estimated [S1], [S3].

**Meta basis mismatch — no variance calculated.** Meta's guidance ($60B–$65B) includes finance-lease principal, whereas the corpus FY2025 actual for Meta is cash paid to acquire property and equipment, which excludes finance-lease principal. Because the bases differ, a percentage variance of the cash-capex actual against Meta's guidance is not like-for-like and must not be calculated [S1], [S3]. For reference only, Meta's company-reported FY2025 capex measure including finance-lease principal was $72.215B ($69.691B cash PP&E plus $2.524B finance-lease principal) [S1], [S3].

## Cross-Company Comparison

**Capex intensity (Capex/OCF), FY2025:** Amazon 94%, Meta 60%, Alphabet 56%, Microsoft 47%, Apple 11%, NVIDIA 5% [S7]. Amazon's ratio previously peaked at 132–136% in FY2021–FY2022 with negative FCF [S7].

**Operating margin, FY2025:** NVIDIA 62.4%, Microsoft 45.6%, Meta 41.4%, Apple 32.0%, Alphabet 32.0%, Amazon 11.2% [S7]. Amazon's margin remains the lowest despite $716.9B revenue, versus NVIDIA at $130.5B revenue [S6].

**Free cash flow, FY2025:** Apple $98.8B, Alphabet $73.3B, Microsoft $71.6B, NVIDIA $60.9B, Meta $46.1B, Amazon $7.7B [S7]. The two highest-margin companies (NVIDIA, Apple) also show the lowest capex intensity; Amazon pairs the lowest margin with the highest intensity [S4], [S6].

**Capex trajectory (FY2020→FY2025):** Amazon $40.1B→$131.8B, Alphabet $22.3B→$91.4B, Meta $15.2B→$69.7B, Microsoft $15.4B→$64.6B, Apple $7.3B→$12.7B; NVIDIA $1.0B→$3.2B (FY2022–FY2025) [S5], [S7].

All comparisons are on a fiscal-year basis; fiscal year-ends differ (Dec, Jun, late Jan, late Sep) [S2], [S7].

## Data Gaps

All required FY2025 actual metrics (revenue, operating income, operating cash flow, capex, FCF, capex intensity) are present for all six companies [S4][S6]. The disclosed gaps relate to guidance, not actuals: no initial FY2025 capex guidance is provided for Microsoft, NVIDIA, or Apple, and these must be reported as unavailable rather than estimated [S3][S7]. Additionally, Meta's corpus actual (cash PP&E) excludes finance-lease principal, creating a basis mismatch versus its guidance; a guidance variance must not be calculated [S3][S7]. NVIDIA lacks Capex and FCF figures for FY2020–FY2021 [S3]. No missing values were inferred or estimated. This pack is limited to the frozen five-source corpus.
