# Junior Analyst Data Pack — FY2025 AI Capex Intensity

## Overview and Definitions

FY2025 denotes each company’s fiscal year 2025, not a common calendar period. Fiscal year-end is December for Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta; June for Microsoft; late January for NVIDIA; and late September for Apple. These differing periods limit direct comparability across companies. [S6]

Revenue and operating income are the corresponding reported financial measures; operating margin equals operating income divided by revenue. OCF means operating cash flow. Capex means capital expenditures, represented in the corpus by purchases of property and equipment. Free cash flow (FCF) is cash remaining after capex: **FCF = OCF − capex**. Capex intensity measures the share of operating cash flow absorbed by investment: **capex intensity = capex / OCF**. Amounts are in US$ billions unless expressed as percentages. [S1] [S6]

## Cross-Company Comparison

Amazon has the largest revenue base among the six companies, followed by Apple and Alphabet, while NVIDIA has the smallest. Conversely, NVIDIA records the highest operating margin and Amazon the lowest; Apple and Alphabet are tied between these extremes [S2][S5]. Alphabet generates the most operating cash flow (OCF), whereas NVIDIA generates the least. Amazon reports both the highest capex and the highest capex/OCF ratio, while NVIDIA has the lowest on both measures [S1][S2]. Free cash flow (FCF) is highest at Apple and lowest at Amazon; Alphabet and Microsoft rank next behind Apple [S1][S2].

These comparisons use FCF defined as OCF less capex and capex intensity as capex divided by OCF [S6]. They are fiscal-year comparisons rather than strictly aligned periods: Amazon, Alphabet and Meta close in December, Microsoft in June, NVIDIA in late January and Apple in late September. Consequently, the FY2025 observations should not be treated as directly calendar-comparable [S2][S6].
