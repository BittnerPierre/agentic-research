# Junior Analyst Data Pack — FY2025 AI Capex Intensity

## Overview and Definitions

FY2025 is defined as the latest complete fiscal year for all six companies in the frozen corpus. Because each company reports on a different fiscal calendar, figures are aligned on a fiscal-year basis rather than a calendar-year basis to avoid period misalignment [S1]. Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta end their fiscal year in December; Microsoft ends in June; NVIDIA ends in late January; and Apple ends in late September [S1]. These conventions are consistently applied across the dataset, and all comparisons in this report are made on this fiscal-year basis [S1].

Two standardized metrics are used throughout this report. **Free cash flow (FCF)** is defined as operating cash flow minus capital expenditures. **Capex intensity** is defined as capital expenditures divided by operating cash flow [S1]. These definitions provide a neutral basis for cross-company comparison despite the differing fiscal-year ends [S1].

A note on scope: this report treats reported FY2025 actuals as the primary basis of analysis. Forward-looking initial FY2025 capex guidance, where available, is treated as a separate historical snapshot and is not conflated with reported actuals [S1].

## Capex Trends

Amazon's capex rose from $40.1B in FY2020 to $131.8B in FY2025, with capex/OCF climbing from 61% to 94%, the highest intensity among the six companies [S7]. Alphabet's capex grew from $22.3B to $91.4B over the same window, with capex/OCF rising from 34% to 56% [S7]. Meta increased capex from $15.2B to $69.7B (FY2020–FY2025), with intensity moving from 39% to 60% [S7].

Microsoft (FYE Jun) also spans FY2020–FY2025, with capex rising from $15.4B to $64.6B and capex/OCF increasing from 25% to 47% [S7]. Apple (FYE Sep) grew capex from $7.3B to $12.7B over FY2020–FY2025, holding capex/OCF at 11% [S7].

NVIDIA (FYE Jan) has a shorter available window: the earliest capex year in the corpus is FY2022, at $1.0B, rising to $3.2B in FY2025, approximately 5% of operating cash flow [S7].

All six companies show rising capex from their earliest available year to FY2025. Amazon is the largest absolute spender and shows the sharpest absolute increase ($91.7B), while NVIDIA and Apple sit at the low end of magnitude ($3.2B and $12.7B respectively) [S7]. Intensity rankings at FY2025 place Amazon highest (94%), followed by Meta (60%), Alphabet (56%), Microsoft (47%), Apple (11%), and NVIDIA lowest (5%) [S6].

## Cross-Company Comparison

Les six sociétés présentent des profils de dépenses d'investissement et de génération de trésorerie nettement hétérogènes pour leur exercice fiscal 2025 (FY2025) [S4]. Une comparaison directe doit toutefois tenir compte de deux limites méthodologiques. Premièrement, les fins d'exercice diffèrent : décembre pour Amazon, Alphabet et Meta ; juin pour Microsoft ; fin janvier pour NVIDIA ; fin septembre pour Apple. Les comparaisons doivent donc s'effectuer sur une base d'exercice fiscal et non calendaire [S4]. Deuxièmement, les bases de reporting ne sont pas strictement identiques, notamment pour Meta dont l'actualité du corpus (cash payé pour les immobilisations corporelles) exclut le principal des contrats de location-financement, contrairement à ses prévisions initiales [S4].

Sur le plan des indicateurs, l'intensité capitalistique (capex/OCF) s'étage de 5 % (NVIDIA) à 94 % (Amazon), avec Meta (60 %), Alphabet (56 %), Microsoft (47 %) et Apple (11 %) entre les deux [S4]. La génération de trésorerie disponible (FCF) varie également fortement : Apple ($98,8 Md) et Alphabet ($73,3 Md) dégagent les FCF les plus élevés, tandis qu'Amazon n'a généré que $7,7 Md [S4]. Ces écarts reflètent des stratégies d'allocation distinctes plutôt qu'une performance uniforme. Enfin, aucune prévision initiale de capex FY2025 n'est disponible pour Microsoft, NVIDIA ou Apple dans le corpus, et les prévisions ne doivent pas être confondues avec les réalisations [S4].

## Data Gaps

FY2025 capex guidance is unavailable for three of the six companies in the frozen source pack: Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Apple [S3]. The guidance file states this explicitly and instructs that it be reported as unavailable rather than estimated [S3][S2]. Initial FY2025 capex guidance exists only for Amazon (~$100B, released 2025-02-06), Alphabet (~$75B, 2025-02-04), and Meta ($60B–$65B, 2025-01-29) [S2].

The gap is specific to guidance, not actuals. FY2025 reported actuals are present for Microsoft (capex $64.6B) and NVIDIA (capex $3.2B) in the reference data [S3][S6]. Apple's FY2025 rows are not returned in the retrieved chunks; the reference data shows Apple only up to FY2024 [S3]. No inference or estimation of missing guidance values has been performed; these should be stated as unavailable [S3].
